# Observe Insurance FAQ (Knowledge Base)

## Office hours
Our claims office is open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time, and Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM. We are closed on Sundays and major holidays.

## Mailing address
Observe Insurance, Claims Department, 500 Market Street, Suite 200, Columbus, OH 43215.

## How to start a new claim
You can start a new claim three ways: online at the Observe Insurance customer portal under "File a Claim", by calling our claims line during office hours to speak with a representative, or by mailing a written description to our claims mailing address. Have your policy number, the date of the incident, and a short description of what happened ready.

## How to submit documents
If your claim needs documents, you can upload them in the customer portal under "My Claims", email them to claims-docs@observeinsurance.example with your claim number in the subject line, or mail copies to our claims mailing address. Please do not send originals.

## General claims process
1. You file the claim.
2. The claim is assigned to an adjuster, usually within 1 business day.
3. The adjuster reviews the claim and may request documents or photos.
4. A decision is made, typically within 7 to 10 business days once all documents are received.
5. If approved, payment is issued within 5 to 7 business days. If denied, you can request a review.

## Things the assistant cannot help with
Coverage disputes, legal advice, payout amounts, and policy changes must be handled by a representative.

## Emergencies
If anyone is injured or in danger, call 911 immediately. This line is not for emergencies.
